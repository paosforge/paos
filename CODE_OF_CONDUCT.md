
# Code of conduct

PaOS is a small project. We want it to be a good place for everyone.

**Please:**
- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Criticize work, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic.
- Respect people's time, including your own.

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, including floods of unchecked AI-written reports

**What happens:** we may remove posts, close threads, or block accounts. For serious cases we act first and explain after.

**To report a problem:** email hello@paosforge.com. Peter reads it. Reports are kept private.

This applies in the repository, in project email, and anywhere you speak for the project.
